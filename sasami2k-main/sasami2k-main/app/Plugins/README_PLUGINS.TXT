
**** Short Directions to use your WINAMP DSP in SASAMI2k ****


1) Put your Winamp DSP files(dsp_*.dll) here,

2) Open your SASAMI's preferences,

3) Select your DSP and Turn on that

4) Press "OK", and now that will work in SASAMI2k.

5) ONLY once you've turned on, you can configure.



ps) do not make sub folders!

ps2) Pitch controls are not supported.

ps3) go Winamp.com - you can get many DSPs

ps4) I know one Normalizing DSP - RockSteady
